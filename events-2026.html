Aug 132026

Create a safe space — with Whatcom Dispute Resolution Center

Ferndale Public Library, 2125 Main St, Ferndale, WA 98248 DetailsClose
When
2:00–3:00 PM
Who
Young people ages 12+ and parents

A Better Way Out and the Whatcom Dispute Resolution Center collaborated to create a safe space — Support. Listen. Respect. Together.

Subject: stress and how to deal with it. Young people and parents learned healthy ways to manage stress, shared and listened in a safe space, built stronger relationships, and grew together as a community.

You matter. Your voice matters. Our community matters. Stronger together — better together!

Learn more about our partner at whatcomdrc.org.

Aug 12026

Amando Mi Comunidad — Loving My Community

4860 Northwest Dr, Bellingham, WA 98226 DetailsClose
When
12:00–3:30 PM
Cost
Free, whole family welcome
Sponsor
Whatcom Community Foundation

Amando Mi Comunidad (Loving My Community) was a free, family-friendly community event held on Saturday, August 1. There was delicious food for everyone and fun activities for kids, while invited organizations shared information about resources, services, and support available for you and your community, including:

  • Mental health counselors
  • Food bank
  • Home weatherization & repair
  • Education specialist
  • Housing services & support
  • Health support
  • And more

Special thanks to sponsor Whatcom Community Foundation for supporting this event.

Jun 262026

Jam on the River 2026 — live music & family festival

Centennial Riverwalk Park, 2007 Cherry St, Ferndale, WA 98248 DetailsClose
When
Fri June 26, 5:00–8:30 PM. Sat June 27, 12:00–8:30 PM
Cost
Free

Jam on the River returned to Ferndale’s Centennial Riverwalk Park on June 26–27, 2026. This free, all-ages festival brought the community together along the Nooksack River for a weekend of live music, food trucks, a beer garden, local vendors, and kids’ activities.

A Better Way Out had a booth at the festival, sharing our programs and mission with families who stopped by to say hi.

May 162026

Homeless outreach

Downtown Bellingham, Cornwall Alleys DetailsClose
When
Met at 12:00 PM
Who
Open to all volunteers

Our team and volunteers gathered to serve those experiencing homelessness in downtown Bellingham. Volunteers met at noon to prepare food together, then headed out to the Cornwall Alleys area to distribute meals and supplies directly to those in need.

A huge thank you to everyone who showed up with a compassionate heart. Your time and presence made a real difference.

Apr 252026

Q&A with the Director

Ferndale Public Library, 2125 Main St, Ferndale, WA 98248 DetailsClose
When
10:30 AM–12:30 PM
Who
Ages 12+

A community Q&A with the Director of A Better Way Out: a chance to learn about our mission, our programs, and how we support people in finding positive paths in life. Attendees asked questions about our mission, met the director and team, and learned how to get involved.

Who attended: youth ages 12 and up, community members, parents and mentors, city officials, nonprofits, and business leaders.
