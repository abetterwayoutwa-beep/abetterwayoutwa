Create a Safe Space — with Whatcom Dispute Resolution Center

📍 Ferndale Public Library — 2125 Main St, Ferndale, WA 98248 🕒 2:00 PM – 3:00 PM 👥 Young People Ages 12+ & Parents Welcome

Why join?

  • Learn healthy ways to manage stress

Amando Mi Comunidad — Loving My Community

📍 4860 Northwest Dr, Bellingham, WA 98226 🕒 12:00 PM – 3:30 PM 🎟️ Free — Whole Family Welcome

Amando Mi Comunidad (Loving My Community) was a free, family-friendly community event held on Saturday, August 1. There was delicious food for everyone and fun activities for kids, while invited organizations shared valuable information about resources, services, and support available for you and your community, including:

  • Mental health counselors
  • Food bank
  • Home weatherization & repair
  • Education specialist
  • Housing services & support
  • Health support
  • And more!

Special thanks to sponsor Whatcom Community Foundation for supporting this event.

Jam on The River 2026 — Live Music & Family Friendly Festival

📍 Centennial Riverwalk Park, 2007 Cherry St., Ferndale, WA 98248 🕒 Fri June 26: 5–8:30 PM  |  Sat June 27: 12–8:30 PM 🎟️ Admission: Free!

Jam on The River returned to Ferndale's Centennial Riverwalk Park on June 26–27, 2026! This free, all-ages festival brought the community together along the beautiful Nooksack River for a weekend of live music, food trucks, a beer garden, local vendors, and kids' activities.

Friday, June 26 | 5:00 PM – 8:30 PM
Live music and beer garden along the river.

Saturday, June 27 | 12:00 PM – 8:30 PM
Vendor booths, kids' activities (12–5 PM), food trucks, live music, and beer garden into the evening.

A Better Way Out had a booth at the festival — come say hi and learn more about our programs and mission!

Find us at our booth during the festival. Follow us on Facebook for updates and schedule info.

Homeless Outreach

📍 Downtown Bellingham — Cornwall Alleys 🕒 Meet at 12:00 PM 🤝 Open to All Volunteers

On May 16th, our team and volunteers gathered to serve those experiencing homelessness in Downtown Bellingham. Volunteers met at 12:00 PM to prepare food together, then headed out to the Cornwall Alleys area to distribute meals and supplies directly to those in need.

A huge thank you to everyone who showed up with a compassionate heart. Your time and presence made a real difference.
