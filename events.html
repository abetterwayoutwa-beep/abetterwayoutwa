2026 Gump Run 5K — Running for the Kids & A Better Way Out

📅 Saturday, October 24, 2026 📍 Everson City Park, 201–205 Lincoln St, Everson, WA 98247 🕒 Check-in 9:00 AM · 5K Start 10:00 AM 🏃 Kids, families, youth, adults & supporters of all ages

Mark your calendars! The 2026 Gump Run 5K is happening on Saturday, October 24, 2026. Lace up your shoes and join us for a fun run supporting the kids and the mission of A Better Way Out. Every step helps us provide mentorship, support, and hope to at-risk youth in our community.

Run. Support. Transform. A community 5K creating paths to hope, opportunity, and a better way out. Check-in opens at 9:00 AM and the 5K starts at 10:00 AM at Everson City Park (201–205 Lincoln St, Everson, WA 98247). Kids, families, youth, adults, and supporters of all ages are welcome.

Ways you can help: register & run, donate, sponsor, or volunteer — every step supports change.

🐾 Please note: no pets allowed — sorry for the inconvenience.
