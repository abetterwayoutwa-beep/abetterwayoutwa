--- title: "Homeless outreach" where: "Downtown Bellingham" recap: text: |

We had a wonderful time blessing people who are underprivileged. We packed lunch bags, loaded up the wagons with food, water, snacks, toiletries, and naloxone, and walked downtown Bellingham to meet people where they are.

God provides everyone's needs, and He sends those who are ready to be sent out. God bless everyone.
